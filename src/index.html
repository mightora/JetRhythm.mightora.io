THE WHY BEHIND THE PLAN

Jet lag is a body clock problem

It's not just a lack of sleep. After a long flight, your internal day can be out of step with the sun.

01☀

Light moves your clock

Bright light can move your body clock earlier or later depending on when your internal night occurs. Wrongly timed light may delay adjustment.

02☾

Darkness matters too

Reducing light at certain times protects the shift you are trying to make. Sleep is important, but sleep timing alone does not fully explain jet lag.

03◷

Make room to adapt

Gradual schedule shifts before departure may help. Your chronotype, recent sleep, natural light and travel conditions will affect the outcome.

Transparent, research-informed, and independent

Our original planning model uses published circadian principles. It estimates body-clock timing from self-reported sleep, without measuring melatonin onset or core body temperature. It is not clinically validated, medical advice, or affiliated with other jet lag applications.

COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things to know

How do I reduce jet lag when travelling east?

Moving your sleep schedule earlier before travel may help. Timed light exposure is important, but the correct time depends on your estimated biological night. For large time changes, simply seeking light early in the morning can be counterproductive.

What about travelling west?

Westward trips usually call for delaying your internal clock. Evening light can help with some shifts, but personalised timing and the number of time zones crossed matter.

Can I look up flights by number?

Enter the number and departure date to find published schedules when the aviation provider is enabled. If live lookup is unavailable, enter the times from your airline ticket. Airport time zones are always selected automatically from a bundled database.

How accurate are the recommendations?

They are approximations based on your usual sleep and the published principles of circadian phase shifting. They cannot measure your internal clock or predict precisely when you will recover. Illness, sleep loss, light exposure and individual differences affect results.

Do I need melatonin or sleeping tablets?

No. JetRhythm does not prescribe medication or generate individual melatonin doses. Ask a qualified clinician or pharmacist before considering it, particularly if you take medicines or have a medical condition.

Do you send my itinerary to a server?

Plan calculations and saved journeys remain local. Only when you select Find this flight do the flight number and departure date go to our Cloudflare Worker and AeroDataBox. Sleep settings and saved journeys do not go to the lookup service. Normal website and shared-asset requests are also visible to their providers. See the privacy information below.

YOUR DEVICE, YOUR DATA

Private by design

JetRhythm requires no account. Trip calculations happen in your browser. Saved journeys are kept locally and can be exported or deleted at any time. No tracking or advertising cookies are set by JetRhythm.

What leaves your browser?

Cloudflare hosts the online site; Mightora shared assets may load from GitHub/jsDelivr. These providers process normal connection requests. Choosing Find this flight sends only the flight number and departure date to a Cloudflare Worker and, if enabled, to AeroDataBox. No sleep settings or saved journeys are sent.
